## Summary Recommendation

**Primary target: European Respiratory Journal (ERJ), Perspective section.**  
**Secondary target: AJRCCM, Perspectives section.**  
**Tertiary / fallback: ERJ Open Research or Chest (Contemporary Review).**

Rationale: The manuscript is a narrative review proposing a conceptual model, not a systematic review or primary data paper. Most high-impact cardiology journals (EHJ, Circulation, JACC main) commission reviews by invitation; unsolicited single-author narrative reviews rarely pass desk screening. The ERJ Perspective format is designed for exactly this kind of argument-driven, evidence-triangulating review. The style exemplar (Heffernan & Rutherford, CJC Open 2025) confirms that mid-tier open-access cardiology journals accept narrative reviews in this register, but the respiratory angle and the two-clock model's novelty place it more naturally in a respiratory journal.

## Submission Strategy

1. **First choice: ERJ.** Submit as a Review article (not Perspective, to avoid word-count cuts). If the editors route it to Perspective, condense §4-6 (already consolidated in Stage 4) and trim §9.1-9.2. The ERS connection (EXACOS-CV papers in ERJ Open Research) strengthens the case.

2. **If ERJ declines: AJRCCM.** Submit as a full Review. If offered Perspective, condense aggressively: merge §1-2, §3-5, §10-12; reduce to 3,000 words with a single table and the central illustration.

3. **If both decline: ERJ Open Research.** No word cut needed. Same ERS editorial standards. Open access APC is the main barrier; ERS may waive for low/middle-income country authors (Indonesia qualifies).

4. **Parallel option: CJC Open.** If speed is a priority and the author is willing to accept a lower impact factor, CJC Open is the safest bet: the exemplar paper confirms acceptance of this exact manuscript type.

5. **Diagnostic corollary split (optional):** §8 (diagnostic corollary) could be extracted as a standalone Viewpoint for a cardiology journal (JACC: Advances or CJC Open), but this would weaken the parent manuscript and is not recommended unless the main paper is rejected.
