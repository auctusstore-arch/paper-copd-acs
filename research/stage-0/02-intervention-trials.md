# Intervention and Trial Evidence

Items: M2, C4, M3, M13

Source-location convention: unless a record names another table or analysis, primary endpoint values come from the PubMed abstract Results section. The verification manifest records whether PMC full text was available or the check was limited to the abstract and DOI metadata.

Record convention: citation and bullets report source facts. The prose after those bullets states the interpretation permitted for the manuscript.

## Influenza vaccination

### IAMI

Fröbert O, Götberg M, Erlinge D, et al. Influenza Vaccination After Myocardial Infarction: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multicenter Trial. *Circulation.* 2021;144(18):1476-1484.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.121.057042
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34459211/
- Population: 2,571 participants, 99.7% recruited after MI.
- Primary endpoint at 12 months: all-cause death, MI, or stent thrombosis.
- Result: 5.3% versus 7.2%, HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.52-0.99, p=0.040.
- Location: PubMed abstract Results.

The trial stopped before the planned sample size during the COVID-19 pandemic. It supports vaccination after MI, but it did not test a COPD-specific effect.

### IVVE

Loeb M, Dokainish H, Dans A, et al. Influenza vaccine to reduce adverse vascular events in patients with heart failure: a multinational randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. *Lancet Glob Health.* 2022;10(12):e1835-e1844.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S2214-109X(22)00432-6
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36400089/
- Population: 5,129 patients with heart failure in low-income and middle-income countries.
- First co-primary composite: 14.8% versus 16.0%, HR 0.93, 95% CI 0.81-1.07, p=0.30.
- Second co-primary recurrent-events outcome: HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.84-1.02, p=0.12.
- Location: PubMed abstract Results.

Neither co-primary outcome showed a statistically significant reduction across the full follow-up. Seasonal analyses suggested a stronger effect during peak influenza circulation, but they do not replace the co-primary results.

### RCT meta-analysis

Behrouzi B, Bhatt DL, Cannon CP, et al. Association of Influenza Vaccination With Cardiovascular Risk: A Meta-analysis. *JAMA Netw Open.* 2022;5(4):e228873.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.8873
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35486404/
- PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9055450/
- Six RCTs, 9,001 patients.
- MACE: 3.6% versus 5.4%, RR 0.66, 95% CI 0.53-0.83.
- Recent ACS subgroup: RR 0.55, 95% CI 0.41-0.75.

This meta-analysis predates the full IVVE result. The manuscript must not describe it as a pooled analysis that includes IVVE.

### Updated meta-analysis including IVVE

Modin D, Lassen MCH, Claggett B, et al. Influenza vaccination and cardiovascular events in patients with ischaemic heart disease and heart failure: A meta-analysis. *Eur J Heart Fail.* 2023;25(9):1685-1692.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/ejhf.2945
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37370193/
- Five peer-reviewed and one non-peer-reviewed RCT, 9,340 patients. The heart-failure trial contributed 5,129 participants, matching IVVE.
- Primary composite: random-effects HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.63-0.88, p<0.001, I2=52%.
- Cardiovascular death: HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.42-0.95.
- All-cause death: HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.54-0.95.
- Location: PubMed abstract Results.

This analysis supplies the post-IVVE synthesis requested in the roadmap. Moderate heterogeneity and the inclusion of one non-peer-reviewed data set require explicit qualification. The authors report similar results after excluding the non-peer-reviewed data.

## Beta-blockers in COPD

### BICS

Devereux G, Cotton S, Nath M, et al. Bisoprolol in Patients With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease at High Risk of Exacerbation: The BICS Randomized Clinical Trial. *JAMA.* 2024;332(6):462-470.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2024.8771
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38762800/
- PMCID: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11322848/
- Population: 519 patients with COPD at high exacerbation risk and no requirement for a cardiovascular indication.
- Primary endpoint: treated COPD exacerbations.
- Result: adjusted incidence-rate ratio 0.97, 95% CI 0.84-1.13, p=0.72.

BICS does not support bisoprolol to prevent COPD exacerbations. It does not justify withholding a beta-blocker when a cardiovascular indication exists.

### PACE

Jenkins CR, Martin A, Chang CL, et al.; PACE Investigators. Bisoprolol to Prevent Adverse Cardiac Events (PACE) in COPD: a Multicentre, Double-Blind, Randomised, Controlled, Phase 3 Trial. *Lancet Respir Med.* 2026;14(3):203-214.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-2600(25)00390-X
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41579873/
- Publication date: online 21 January 2026; print March 2026.
- Population: 280 patients with COPD, FEV1 30% to 70% predicted, and prior exacerbation.
- Primary hierarchical composite: death, cardiac or respiratory hospitalisation, exacerbation, quality of life, and FEV1.
- Result: win ratio 0.95, 95% CI 0.72-1.25, p=0.72.

PACE was not a 2025 publication. The neutral result concerns broad cardiopulmonary prevention in COPD, not treatment after ACS.

## Beta-blockers after myocardial infarction

### ABYSS

Silvain J, Cayla G, Ferrari E, et al. Beta-Blocker Interruption or Continuation after Myocardial Infarction. *N Engl J Med.* 2024;391(14):1277-1286.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2404204
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39213187/
- Population: 3,698 stable long-term beta-blocker users after MI, LVEF at least 40%.
- Primary endpoint: death, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, or cardiovascular hospitalisation.
- Result: 23.8% after interruption versus 21.1% with continuation. Noninferiority was not met.

ABYSS concerns interruption of established treatment, not initiation at discharge.

### REBOOT

Ibanez B, Latini R, Rossello X, et al. Beta-Blockers after Myocardial Infarction without Reduced Ejection Fraction. *N Engl J Med.* 2025;393(19):1889-1900.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2504735
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40888702/
- Population: 8,438 patients with STEMI or NSTEMI, invasive care, LVEF greater than 40%.
- Primary endpoint: all-cause death, reinfarction, or heart-failure hospitalisation.
- Result: HR 1.04, 95% CI 0.89-1.22, p=0.63.

### BETAMI-DANBLOCK

Munkhaugen J, Kristensen AMD, Halvorsen S, et al. Beta-Blockers after Myocardial Infarction in Patients without Heart Failure. *N Engl J Med.* 2025;393(19):1901-1911.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2505985
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40888716/
- Population: 5,574 patients with MI, LVEF at least 40%, and no heart failure.
- Primary endpoint: all-cause death or a broad MACE composite.
- Result: 14.2% versus 16.3%, HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.75-0.98, p=0.03.

REBOOT and BETAMI-DANBLOCK differ in population details and endpoint definitions. Their results should not be presented as identical experiments with irreconcilable answers.

## Anti-inflammatory trials

### CANTOS

Ridker PM, Everett BM, Thuren T, et al. Antiinflammatory Therapy with Canakinumab for Atherosclerotic Disease. *N Engl J Med.* 2017;377(12):1119-1131.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1707914
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28845751/
- Population: 10,061 patients with prior MI and hsCRP at least 2 mg/L.
- Primary endpoint for canakinumab 150 mg: HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.74-0.98, p=0.021.
- Important harm: fatal infection or sepsis increased.

### CIRT

Ridker PM, Everett BM, Pradhan A, et al. Low-Dose Methotrexate for the Prevention of Atherosclerotic Events. *N Engl J Med.* 2019;380(8):752-762.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809798
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30415610/
- Population: 4,786 patients with prior MI or multivessel disease plus type 2 diabetes or metabolic syndrome.
- Result: HR 0.96, 95% CI 0.79-1.16, p=0.67.
- Methotrexate did not lower IL-1beta, IL-6, or hsCRP.

### COLCOT

Tardif JC, Kouz S, Waters DD, et al. Efficacy and Safety of Low-Dose Colchicine after Myocardial Infarction. *N Engl J Med.* 2019;381(26):2497-2505.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1912388
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31733140/
- Population: 4,745 patients enrolled within 30 days after MI.
- Primary endpoint: 5.5% versus 7.1%, HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.61-0.96, p=0.02.
- Pneumonia was reported more often with colchicine.

### LoDoCo2

Nidorf SM, Fiolet ATL, Mosterd A, et al. Colchicine in Patients with Chronic Coronary Disease. *N Engl J Med.* 2020;383(19):1838-1847.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2021372
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32865380/
- Population: 5,522 patients with chronic coronary disease after a tolerance run-in.
- Result: 6.8% versus 9.6%, HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.57-0.83, p<0.001.

These trials show pathway and phenotype specificity. CANTOS and colchicine trials support causal inflammatory pathways in selected coronary populations. CIRT shows that a generic anti-inflammatory label does not predict cardiovascular benefit. None establishes a COPD-specific treatment interaction.

## Oxygen in ACS

### DETO2X-AMI

Hofmann R, James SK, Jernberg T, et al. Oxygen Therapy in Suspected Acute Myocardial Infarction. *N Engl J Med.* 2017;377(13):1240-1249.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1706222
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28844200/
- Population: 6,629 patients with suspected MI and oxygen saturation at least 90%.
- Primary endpoint, one-year all-cause death: 5.0% versus 5.1%, HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.79-1.21, p=0.80.

The result applies to routine oxygen in patients without baseline hypoxaemia. It does not support withholding oxygen from a hypoxaemic patient with COPD or ACS.

### COPD subgroup publication

Andell P, James S, Ostlund O, et al. Oxygen therapy in suspected acute myocardial infarction and concurrent normoxemic chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: a prespecified subgroup analysis from the DETO2X-AMI trial. *Eur Heart J Acute Cardiovasc Care.* 2020;9(8):984-992.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/2048872619848978
- PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31081342/

This peer-reviewed paper is a stronger disease-specific source than Andell's 2016 Lund thesis when discussing oxygen in normoxaemic COPD with suspected MI.
