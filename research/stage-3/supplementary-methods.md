# Supplementary Methods

## Search strategy

We searched PubMed, Embase, and the Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials for studies published from inception through July 2026. Search terms combined Medical Subject Headings and free-text terms for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD, chronic obstructive airway disease, emphysema, chronic bronchitis) with terms for cardiovascular disease (myocardial infarction, acute coronary syndrome, heart failure, arrhythmia, pulmonary embolism, cardiovascular events, cardiovascular mortality), and terms for exacerbation, inflammation, and Mendelian randomisation. We also searched reference lists of included studies and recent narrative reviews for additional sources.

Search strings (PubMed):
- ("pulmonary disease, chronic obstructive"[MeSH] OR COPD OR "chronic obstructive") AND ("myocardial infarction"[MeSH] OR "acute coronary syndrome" OR ACS OR "cardiovascular disease"[MeSH]) AND ("exacerbation"[MeSH] OR exacerbation OR trigger)
- ("pulmonary disease, chronic obstructive"[MeSH] OR COPD) AND ("Mendelian randomisation" OR "Mendelian randomization" OR "genetic instrument") AND ("cardiovascular"[Title/Abstract] OR "coronary"[Title/Abstract])
- ("beta blocker"[Title/Abstract] OR "beta-blocker" OR bisoprolol OR metoprolol) AND ("pulmonary disease, chronic obstructive"[MeSH] OR COPD) AND ("myocardial infarction"[MeSH] OR infarction)

Inclusion criteria: original research articles, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, randomised controlled trials, and authoritative guidelines published in English. Narrative reviews were included for framing assessment only and were not used as primary evidence sources. No date restriction was applied beyond the database search date.

Exclusion criteria: conference abstracts without full publication (except where the abstract is the only available source, clearly labelled as such), letters without primary data, editorials, and non-English publications.

## Quality assessment

This narrative review adheres to the SANRA (Scale for the Assessment of Narrative Review Articles) criteria (Baethge et al. 2019 [67]). We report the rationale (Item 1), specific aims (Item 2), literature search strategy (Item 3), referencing of key sources (Item 4), evidence levels (Item 5), and relevant endpoint data (Item 6) for each major claim. The full search strategy is provided above.

For each key claim, we report all eligible studies including those not supporting the thesis. Key claims and their evidence base:

1. COPD is an independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease: Mendelian randomisation studies [7-11], observational cohorts [12, 23-27].
2. Exacerbations trigger acute cardiovascular events: self-controlled case series [12, 15, 16, 23], prospective cohorts [24-27].
3. Beta-blocker benefit tracks cardiac indication, not COPD severity: RCTs [38-46], observational studies [41-43].
4. Influenza vaccination reduces cardiovascular events: RCTs [49, 50], meta-analyses [51, 52].
5. Anti-inflammatory agents with pathway-specific efficacy reduce cardiovascular events: RCTs [59-62].
6. Pulmonary embolism is a competitor to the coronary narrative in AECOPD: systematic review [63], cohort study [64].

## Data availability

All data supporting the claims in this review are available from the cited published sources. The population attributable fraction calculation (Section 7.1) uses published incidence rate ratios from Rothnie et al. (2018) [12] and standard COPD exacerbation rates from the GOLD report [1]. The calculation script is available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.

## Compliance with reporting standards

This review was prepared in accordance with the SANRA quality criteria for narrative reviews. PRISMA reporting guidelines do not apply because this is not a systematic review with a formal meta-analysis. The search strategy is documented above for reproducibility.
