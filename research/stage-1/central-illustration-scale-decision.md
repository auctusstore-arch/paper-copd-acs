# Binding decision for Central Illustration Panel C

Issue: #4
Checklist item: C2

## Decision

Panel C must not place the following estimates on one quantitative axis:

- observational relative risks comparing patients with and without COPD;
- source-reported exponentiated estimates from COPD genetic-liability analyses;
- odds ratios per standard-deviation change in FEV1 or FVC;
- incidence rate ratios for post-exacerbation risk windows.

These estimates use different populations, exposure definitions, timescales and denominators. Their numerical magnitudes are not directly comparable.

## Stage 1 treatment

Panel C is replaced with three nonquantitative lanes for observational, genetic and acute-trigger evidence. The lanes show the question and main limitation of each design without plotting magnitudes on a shared axis. The original raster remains in the DOCX as `image1.png`; its drawing is tracked as a deletion. The revised raster is stored as `image2.png`; its drawing is tracked as an insertion. Rejecting all revisions therefore restores the supplied artwork.

## Remaining Stage 4 presentation work

Use separate visual lanes for:

1. conventional observational association;
2. genetic-liability and lung-function MR estimates;
3. post-exacerbation self-controlled estimates.

The Stage 1 panel establishes the required separation. Stage 4 may simplify typography and density across the full illustration, but it must retain the lane separation and must not imply that proximity to 1.0 has the same clinical meaning across designs.
